# Survival AI privacy policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Survival AI is an offline emergency and survival app for Android, made by Bryan Dietz. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Survival AI does not collect, store or share your personal information. Everything you type or save stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone

- **Chat questions and AI answers.** The AI runs entirely on your phone. Your questions are never sent to us or to any server.
- **Your location.** The app uses your phone's GPS to show where you are on the map, measure distances and plan routes. Your location is used only on the phone, only while the app is open, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Saved spots, family plan and medical ID.** Names, blood types, allergies, medicines, contacts and meeting points you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Language and settings.** Stored only on your phone.

## Downloads

When you choose to download an AI model or a map, the app connects to the server that hosts the file:

- AI models are downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co).
- Maps are downloaded from GitHub (github.com).

As with any download, those servers receive your device's IP address and basic connection information. They handle it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive it. No personal information from the app is sent with these downloads.

## Permissions

- **Location:** to show your position on the offline map. Optional; the rest of the app works without it.
- **Internet:** only to download AI models and maps when you ask.
- **Camera / flashlight:** only to turn on the phone's light for the flashlight and SOS signal.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Medical information

Survival AI gives general first-aid and survival information. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or emergency services.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
